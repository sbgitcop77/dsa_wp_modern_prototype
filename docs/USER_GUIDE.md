## 1.2 Booking a Session

Go to **Book a Session** on the website (header button or any CTA button on the page). The booking process has 5 steps.

### Step 1 — Choose an Instructor

Browse the available coaches and click **Select** on the one you'd like to train with. Each card shows the coach's name, speciality, and a short bio.

**Tip:** If a coach is not shown, they may not be currently active. Contact us at **(443) 865-1639** if you have a specific request.

### Step 2 — Select Date & Time

1. **Pick a date** from the calendar. Only dates when your chosen coach is available are shown. Greyed-out dates are either in the past, a facility closure, or a date when the coach is unavailable. If you go back and change your coach, your selected date and time are cleared — the new coach may have different availability.
2. **Select duration** — 30 minutes or 60 minutes. Choose this first; the available time slots update based on your choice.
3. **Pick a time slot** from the list. Only slots that fit within the coach's session window for that day are shown.

**Waitlist slots:** If a time slot shows in amber with a "Waitlist" label, your chosen coach is already booked by another athlete at that time. You can still select it to join the waitlist — if the conflicting booking is cancelled, admin can promote your session to confirmed.

### Step 3 — Session Frequency

Choose how often you'd like to train:

| Option | Description |
|---|---|
| **Single Session** | One session on the date and time you selected. |
| **Recurring Series** | The same day and time every week for 2, 4, 6, or 8 weeks. |

For recurring sessions, we check each future date automatically. See §1.3 for details.

Click **Next** to continue. If there is a problem with any date in your series, you'll see a message here before proceeding.

### Step 4 — Participant Details

Tell us who the session is for:

- **Myself** — you are the athlete. Fill in your first name, last name, email, and phone number.
- **Another Athlete** — you're booking on behalf of someone else (a child, student, or other athlete). Fill in the athlete's name, age, your relationship to them, and your own contact details.

Your email is used to look up your account. If you've booked before, your details are already on file.

There's also an optional **Additional Instructions** field for notes to the instructor (injuries, goals, areas to focus on). If filled in, it's visible to admin staff on the Today's Schedule grid and in the Manage Bookings detail modal.

### Step 5 — Review & Confirm

Review all the details. If everything looks right, click **Confirm Booking**.

You'll see a confirmation screen with your **booking reference** (format: `DSA-YYYYMMDD-NNNN`, e.g. `DSA-20260711-4823`). Save this — you'll need it to manage your booking later. A confirmation email will be sent to the address you provided.

The confirmation email contains a **manage booking link**. Click that link at any time to cancel or reschedule without needing a login.

## 1.3 Recurring (Weekly) Sessions

When you choose **Recurring Series**, we automatically check every upcoming date in your series before you confirm. Here's what can happen:

### All weeks clear

Every date in your series is available → all sessions are booked as **Confirmed**. Your confirmation screen shows each session (Session 1 of N, Session 2 of N, etc.) with its date and a personal manage link.

### Facility closed on a future date (hard block)

If one of your weekly dates falls on a facility closure (holiday, maintenance day, etc.), you'll see a **red alert** on Step 3 listing the affected date(s). You cannot proceed with that number of weeks. Options:
- Reduce the number of weeks so the series ends before the closure.
- Start your series on a different base date.
- Choose a single session instead.

**Important:** When a hard block exists, the red alert is the only message shown on Step 3. Coach conflicts on other weeks in the same series are not evaluated or displayed until the hard block is resolved.

### Coach conflict on a future date (waitlisted)

If your coach is already booked by another athlete on one of your weekly dates (but the facility itself is open and there is no hard block on any other week), that session is placed on the **waitlist** rather than blocking your entire series. You'll see an **amber notice** on Step 3 showing which date is affected. You can still proceed — the other sessions are confirmed, and the waitlisted session is created in your account.

See §1.9 for details on what waitlisted means and how to confirm it.

## 1.5 Managing Your Booking

Every booking has a dedicated management page. The link to this page is in your confirmation email. You can also construct it directly:

```
https://thediamondsportsacademy.com/manage/[manage-link-from-your-email]
```

The manage link is unique to each booking and requires no login. Keep the link from your confirmation email — it's your personal access to manage that specific session.

On this page you can:
- View full booking details (coach, date, time, duration, status)
- Cancel the session
- Reschedule to a different date or time
- For recurring series: see all sessions in your series and their current status
- For waitlisted sessions: confirm the session when the slot opens

**Lane assignments are not shown on the customer manage page.** Lane numbers are internal — they are visible to admin staff only.

## 1.6 Cancelling a Session

From your booking management page:

1. Click **Cancel — Remove this session**
2. For recurring series, choose:
   - **This session only** — only this date is cancelled; all other sessions remain as scheduled.
   - **Entire series (all upcoming sessions)** — this and all future sessions in the series are cancelled.
3. Add an optional reason.
4. Click **Confirm Cancellation**.

You'll see a confirmation message. A cancellation email is sent to your contact address.

### 24-Hour Change Window

Cancellations and reschedules **must be made more than 24 hours before your session**. For example, a session at 9 AM on Tuesday can be changed up until 9 AM on Monday — after that point the change options are locked and you'll see a "Change Window Closed" notice with instructions to contact us directly:

> **Phone:** (443) 865-1639
> **Hours:** Mon–Fri 8 AM–8 PM, Sat 9 AM–5 PM

**Exception:** If your session is **waitlisted** (not yet confirmed), the 24-hour time lock does not apply. You can confirm or cancel a waitlisted session at any time, even if it starts within 24 hours.

## 1.7 Rescheduling a Session

From your booking management page:

1. Click **Reschedule — Pick a new date & time**
2. Select a new date from the calendar. The calendar shows only dates when your coach is available (blackout dates and dates with no coach availability are disabled).
3. Select a new time slot. Slots already booked by your coach, slots that don't fit within the session window, and slots where you'd extend past the coach's available hours are automatically excluded.
4. Click **Confirm Reschedule**.

**Note:** The session duration (30 or 60 minutes) cannot be changed during a reschedule. Contact us if you need to switch durations.

**Note:** The coach cannot be changed during a self-service reschedule. The manage page always rescheduled with the original coach. To switch coaches, contact us directly.

**Note for recurring series:** The manage page reschedules a single occurrence only. Rescheduling an entire series is not available online — contact us to arrange that.

**Collision prevention:** If you are rescheduling a session within a recurring series, any date and time where your coach already has a confirmed booking is automatically excluded from the slot list. This prevents two of your series sessions landing at the exact same time. You can, however, reschedule to a different time slot on the same date as another series session.

## 1.8 Understanding Your Booking Status

| Status | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Confirmed** | Your session is booked and confirmed. Show up at the scheduled time. |
| **Waitlisted** | Your session exists in our system but the exact time slot has a temporary conflict (coach is booked by another athlete that week). You need to confirm it once the slot opens. See §1.9. |
| **Cancelled** | The session was cancelled by you or by our staff. |
| **Completed** | The session took place. |
| **No Show** | The session date passed without attendance. Repeated no-shows are tracked by admin and may lead to your account being flagged. |

## 1.9 Waitlisted Sessions

A **Waitlisted** session is created whenever a time slot you select has your chosen coach already booked by another athlete. This applies to both single sessions (when you pick an amber slot on Step 2) and recurring series (when one of your future weekly dates conflicts with the coach's existing schedule). In all cases a booking record with status "Waitlisted" is created immediately and a manage link is issued — there is no version of a waitlist request that exists without a booking record.

**What you can do:**

1. **Wait** — if the conflicting booking gets cancelled, our staff may reach out to let you know the slot is available.
2. **Confirm it yourself** — go to your booking management page for the waitlisted session. If the slot is now free, you'll see a green **"Slot Available — Confirm Now"** banner and a **"Confirm This Session"** button. Click it to lock in the session.
3. **Reschedule it** — use the Reschedule option to move the session to a different available date/time.
4. **Cancel it** — if you no longer need it, cancel the single session without affecting your other series sessions.

**Important:** A waitlisted session is NOT guaranteed until you or our staff confirms it. The 24-hour change window does not apply — you can confirm a waitlisted session at any time.

## 1.10 Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Can I book if I don't have an account?**
A: Yes. The first time you book, your email creates a customer record automatically. Future bookings with the same email are linked to your account. Email matching is case-insensitive — `Jane@Example.COM` and `jane@example.com` are treated as the same account.

**Q: What if the coach I want isn't available on the date I need?**
A: Each coach sets their own availability schedule. Try a different date, or choose another coach. You can also contact us directly and we'll do our best to accommodate.

**Q: Can I change the duration during a reschedule?**
A: Not online. Contact us at (443) 865-1639 to change the duration on an existing booking.

**Q: What happens if the facility is closed on one of my recurring session dates?**
A: You'll be shown the blocked date during booking (Step 3) and prevented from confirming. Adjust your number of weeks or start date to avoid the closure.

**Q: Can I book a recurring series that includes holidays?**
A: Only if the holiday is not a designated facility closure. If a session date lands on a blackout date (facility closure), you must adjust the series to avoid it.

**Q: What if I have a waitlisted session but the slot never opens?**
A: You can reschedule the waitlisted session to any other available date, or cancel it without affecting your other series sessions.

**Q: How do I know my cancellation went through?**
A: You'll see a confirmation message on screen and receive a cancellation email. You can also revisit your booking management page — the status will show as "Cancelled".

**Q: What is the cancellation policy?**
A: Cancellations and reschedules must be made more than 24 hours in advance. Within 24 hours, please contact us directly at (443) 865-1639.

**Q: I booked for another athlete — where do I see that on the booking?**
A: On your booking management page, the booking details section shows the athlete's name and age. Your name appears as the booking contact.

**Q: Can I join the waitlist if my coach is already booked?**
A: Yes. When a time slot appears in amber with a "Waitlist" label, your coach is already booked by another athlete at that time. Select it and complete the booking wizard — a waitlisted booking record is created and you'll have a manage link. If the conflicting booking is cancelled, admin can promote your session to confirmed.

**Q: I completed most of the booking form but got an error at the end of Step 4 saying "We're unable to process bookings for this account." What does that mean?**
A: Your account has been flagged or deactivated by our staff. Flagged and inactive accounts cannot complete new online bookings. Please contact us directly at (443) 865-1639 so we can assist you.

## 2.3 Bookings

### Tabs

| Tab | Shows |

| Past | All bookings with a date before today |

Narrow the list by:

- **Status** — Confirmed, Waitlisted, Cancelled, No Show, Completed
- **Date range** — from date / to date

### Booking Detail Modal

Click any row to open the detail modal. It shows:

- Lane number (only shown for Lane Instructor bookings — not shown for Non-Lane Instructors)

**Actions in the modal:**

| Button | When it appears | What it does |
|---|---|---|

The admin reschedule form:
1. **Instructor** — displayed as read-only. Instructor reassignment is not supported. To move a session to a different coach, cancel the booking and create a new one with the correct instructor.

3. **Time slot list** — shows only the instructor's available slots for that date; conflicts with their existing bookings and lane capacity limits are excluded

For recurring bookings, a scope notice is shown. Admin rescheduling via the modal always reschedules **this session only**. To reschedule an entire series, cancel the series and re-book.

### Cancel (Admin)

For recurring bookings, admin can choose:
- **This session only** — only the selected booking is cancelled
- **All remaining sessions in this series** — all bookings with the same series ID from today onward are cancelled

Admin cancellations send a cancellation notification to the customer.

Use the **Export CSV** button to download a CSV of the currently-filtered bookings. Useful for external reporting or record-keeping.

## 2.4 Customers

Go to **Customers** in the sidebar.

The list shows all customer records with their contact info, booking history counts, and status indicators.

### Searching & Filtering

- **Search bar** — search by customer name or email
- **Status filter** — All, Active Only, or Inactive Only
- **Flag filter** — All, Flagged Only, or Not Flagged

### Customer Flags

| Indicator | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Flagged** | Customer has been flagged — either manually by admin or automatically when no-show count reaches 2 or late cancellation count reaches 3. Flagged customers are blocked at Step 4 of the booking wizard — they can navigate the wizard but cannot advance past Participant Details. |
| **Inactive** | Customer account is deactivated. Inactive customers are blocked at Step 4 of the booking wizard in the same way. |
| **SMS Opt-Out** | Customer will not receive SMS reminders. Email notifications still send normally. |

### Actions

Click a customer row to open their detail panel. From there you can:

- **Flag / Unflag** — toggle the flag status. Use this to mark customers with behavioral concerns (repeated no-shows, late cancellations, etc.). Flagged customers are blocked from making new online bookings.
- **Deactivate / Reactivate** — deactivating records the deactivation timestamp and cancels all of the customer's upcoming confirmed and waitlisted bookings. A cancellation notification is sent for each affected booking. Use this for customers who should no longer have access to the platform. **Reactivating a customer restores their account access but does NOT restore the bookings that were cancelled — those must be manually re-booked.**
- **Edit details** — update name, phone, email.
- **Increment no-show count** — record that a customer did not attend a session. The current count is shown on the button. If the count reaches 2 and the customer is not already flagged, they are automatically flagged.
- **Increment late cancellation count** — record a late cancellation. If the count reaches 3 and the customer is not already flagged, they are automatically flagged.
- **Toggle SMS Opt-Out** — enable or disable SMS reminders for this customer.

**Important:** A customer's email is their unique identifier. The system uses it to link future bookings to their existing account. Changing a customer's email may affect future bookings if the customer re-books using their old address.

### Activity Summary

The customer detail panel shows an Activity Summary card with:
- **Flagged** — Yes / No (highlighted red if flagged)
- **No-Shows** — current count (highlighted red at 2 or above)
- **Late Cancellations** — current count (highlighted red at 3 or above)

### Booking History

The customer detail panel shows a full list of all past and upcoming bookings for that customer with status and reference numbers.

## 2.5 Instructors

Go to **Instructors** in the sidebar. The page has two tabs: **Instructors** and **Availability**.

### Instructors Tab

The Instructors tab is a sortable table. Each row shows:
- Name (with initials avatar), email, and phone
- Speciality
- Type: **Lane** or **Non-Lane**
- Status: Active / Inactive

**Lane vs Non-Lane Instructor:**
- **Lane Instructor** — sessions are assigned to a physical batting lane. Lane capacity checks apply. Overbooking a time slot with 4 or more confirmed Lane Instructor bookings creates a waitlist slot for new bookings.
- **Non-Lane Instructor** — sessions do not consume a batting lane (e.g. speed/agility coaching, strategy sessions). Lane capacity checks do not apply.

### Creating an Instructor

Click **+ Add Instructor**. All fields are required:
- First Name, Last Name
- Email (must be unique)
- Phone
- Speciality (e.g. "Pitching & Conditioning")
- **Instructor Type** — Lane or Non-Lane. This affects all capacity calculations and cannot be changed after creation.

### Editing an Instructor

Click the edit icon on the instructor card. You can update all fields except Instructor Type.

### Deactivating an Instructor

Deactivated instructors no longer appear in the public booking wizard. Their historical bookings remain intact. **Existing confirmed bookings are not cancelled automatically** — review the instructor's upcoming bookings in the Bookings tab and handle them manually before deactivating.

## 2.6 Instructor Availability

Go to **Instructors** → click the **Availability** button on an instructor row, or go to **Instructors** → the **Availability** tab.

### Recurring Weekly Template

Set the instructor's default weekly schedule. For each day of the week, toggle it on or off and set the start and end time for the day. This acts as the fallback schedule for any date that has no specific override.

### Date-Specific Overrides

The calendar view shows the current and upcoming weeks. Each date card shows the instructor's availability for that day (from the weekly template or a custom override).

**To override a date:**
1. Click the date card.
2. Toggle the instructor as available or unavailable for that date.
3. If available: set the start and end time.
4. Save.

**Availability rules:**
- Past dates cannot be edited.
- For today: start time cannot be in the past.
- End time must be after start time.
- Blackout dates are shown in red and cannot be edited.
- Saturday hours must fall within 9 AM–5 PM (Saturday facility hours).

**Narrowing availability does not affect existing confirmed bookings.** If you shorten an instructor's availability window for a date that already has confirmed bookings, those bookings remain confirmed. The narrowed window only restricts new bookings going forward.

**Apply to all active days:** Copies the current date's start/end time to all other active, editable dates in the same calendar month. Saturday's 9 AM–5 PM facility constraint still applies — if the hours being copied would violate that limit for a Saturday date, that Saturday is skipped.

**Frozen dates:** If a date shows a lock icon, it is frozen and cannot be edited through the normal flow. Contact your system administrator to unfreeze it.

### How Availability Drives the Booking Wizard

When a customer opens the public booking wizard and selects a coach:
- Only dates with availability records show as selectable in the calendar
- Time slots shown are the 30-minute intervals from the availability window start to end
- A slot is hidden if the session duration would extend past the window end time
- A slot is hidden if the instructor already has a confirmed booking overlapping that time

## 2.7 Waitlist

Go to **Waitlist** in the sidebar.

The waitlist page shows all bookings with status "Waitlisted" — these are sessions where the chosen coach was already booked by another athlete at the requested time. Each entry has a booking record and manage link. Entries are grouped by instructor + date + start time so admin can see how many customers are waiting on the same slot.

### Tabs

**Upcoming** — waitlist entries for today and future dates (actions available).
**Past** — entries for dates that have already passed (read-only).

### Actions

- **Confirm** — changes the booking from Waitlisted to Confirmed. A confirmation notification (email + SMS + calendar) is sent to the customer. The entry is removed from the waitlist view.
- **Remove** — cancels the waitlisted booking and removes the entry. No notification is sent to the customer when an entry is removed this way.

**Lane openings do not trigger automatic promotion.** If a confirmed booking is cancelled and a lane frees up, the system does not automatically promote any Lane Unavailable waitlist entries for that slot. Admin must check the Waitlist tab and manually promote eligible entries.

### Searching

Use the search box to filter entries by customer name.

## 2.8 Academy Schedule

Go to **Academy Schedule** (or **Schedule**) in the sidebar.

Configures when the facility is open and which dates are closed.

### Operating Hours

A table with one row per day of the week (Monday through Sunday).

**Each row contains:**
- **Open/Closed toggle** — if Closed, no bookings are accepted on this day and instructor availability cannot be set for that weekday
- **Open Time** — start of facility hours
- **Close Time** — end of facility hours

Click **Save Changes** to apply.

**Conflict detection:** If the new hours conflict with existing confirmed bookings (e.g. closing at 5 PM when a booking exists at 6 PM), an **Affected Bookings** table appears before saving. Review the conflicts and confirm to proceed.

**Important — "Closed" day behavior:** Marking a day as Closed prevents new bookings and prevents new instructor availability from being set for that weekday. Existing instructor availability records for future dates on that day are not automatically deleted — they are simply ignored for booking purposes while the day remains Closed. Existing confirmed bookings on that day are also not automatically cancelled; they appear in the conflict detection table so you can handle them manually.

**Important — Changing hours does not adjust instructor availability:** Reducing or expanding operating hours (e.g. cutting Saturday to 10 AM–2 PM) does not automatically update any instructor's configured availability window. Instructor availability must be adjusted separately in the Instructors → Availability tab. Confirmed bookings that fall outside the new hours appear in the conflict detection table.

**Default hours:**
- Monday–Friday: 6 AM–Midnight
- Saturday: 9 AM–5 PM
- Sunday: Closed

### Blackout Dates

Dates when the facility is closed facility-wide. No bookings can be created for any instructor on a blackout date.

**To add a blackout date:**
1. Click **+ Add Blackout Date**
2. Choose the date
3. Enter a reason (e.g. "Annual Deep Clean")
4. Toggle **Recurring annually** if this closure repeats every year on the same calendar date (e.g. a holiday)
5. Save

Before saving, the system shows an **Affected Bookings** preview listing any confirmed bookings on that date. Review these before confirming — you will need to handle those bookings separately (contact affected customers, cancel or reschedule in the Bookings tab).

**To remove a blackout:** Click the delete icon next to the entry. The date immediately becomes bookable again. Removing a blackout does NOT automatically promote any waitlisted bookings on that date — those must be handled manually in the Waitlist tab.

**Rules:**
- Blackout dates cannot be added in the past.
- Recurring blackouts match by month and day only — the year is ignored. A recurring blackout on July 4 will block July 4 in every future year.

## 2.10 Notifications

Go to **Notifications** in the sidebar.

This is a log of all notifications the system has sent (or attempted to send). Each entry shows:
- Type (confirmation, reminder, cancellation, etc.)
- Channel (email, SMS, calendar)
- Recipient (customer or admin)
- Sent time
- Delivery status: **Sent**, **Pending**, or **Failed**

**Note:** In the current prototype, notifications are recorded but not actually sent. In production, this log reflects real email and SMS delivery.

### Summary Cards

At the top of the page:
- **Sent** — count of successfully delivered notifications
- **Pending** — count of notifications queued but not yet sent
- **Failed** — count of notifications that failed to deliver

### Filters

- **Search** — by booking reference or recipient name
- **Status filter** — All, Sent, Pending, Failed
- **Type filter** — All Types, or a specific notification type

### Notification Types

| Type | When it fires |
|---|---|
| Confirmation | New booking created and confirmed; waitlisted booking promoted to confirmed |
| Waitlist | Booking created with waitlisted status (instructor conflict in a recurring series) |
| Change | Booking rescheduled (by customer or admin) |
| Cancellation | Booking cancelled (by customer or admin, single or entire series) |
| Reminder (24hr) | 24 hours before session — requires scheduled job in production |
| Reminder (2hr SMS) | 2 hours before session — requires scheduled job in production |

### Channel Rules

| Event | Customer channels | Admin channels |
|---|---|---|
| Confirmation | Email + SMS + Calendar invite | Email only |
| Waitlist | Email + SMS | Email only |
| Cancellation | Email + SMS | Email only |
| Change / Reschedule | Email + SMS | Email only |

> **Why no calendar invite for admin?** The admin has a real-time full view of all bookings through the Dashboard and Bookings pages. Calendar invites go to customers only — they have no app access and benefit from the event in their personal calendar.

> **Instructors receive no automated notifications.** The notification system sends to customers and admin only. Instructor schedules are managed through the admin Instructors → Availability tab.

> **SMS opt-out:** If a customer has opted out of SMS, the SMS channel is skipped for all notification types. Email and calendar channels still fire normally.

### Retry

For failed or pending notifications, click **Retry** to re-queue delivery.

### Actions That Create Notifications

- Customer books a confirmed session → confirmation (email + SMS + calendar to customer + email to admin)
- Customer books a waitlisted session → waitlist notification (email + SMS to customer + email to admin)
- Customer cancels on the manage page → cancellation (email + SMS to customer + email to admin)
- Customer reschedules on the manage page → change (email + SMS to customer + email to admin)
- Admin cancels a booking → cancellation (email + SMS to customer + email to admin)
- Admin reschedules a booking → change (email + SMS to customer + email to admin)
- Admin confirms a waitlisted booking → confirmation (email + SMS + calendar to customer + email to admin)
- Admin deactivates a customer → cancellation per affected future booking (email + SMS to customer + email to admin per booking)

## 2.11 Settings

Go to **Settings** in the sidebar. Settings has five tabs: General, Lane Configuration, Notifications, Cancellation Policy, and Security.

> **Note:** Operating Hours and Blackout Dates are managed in **Academy Schedule** (§2.8), not in Settings.

### General (Tab 1)

Update the facility's name, address, phone, email, and website. These values appear on public-facing pages, notifications, and throughout the admin panel.

### Lane Configuration (Tab 2)

The **Active Lanes** setting controls how many simultaneous lane sessions can be booked at the same time slot.

- Default: 4 lanes
- Accepted range: 1–10

Increasing lane count: takes effect immediately for new bookings.

**Decreasing lane count:** If there are confirmed upcoming bookings assigned to lanes above the new count (e.g. reducing from 4 to 3 when a booking is on lane 4), a conflict warning appears before saving. The warning lists every affected booking.

You have two options:
- **Cancel** — dismisses the warning; no changes are saved. Use this to review the affected bookings first in the Bookings tab.
- **Save Anyway** — saves the new lane count. Listed bookings remain confirmed on their current lanes. You must manually review and handle them. No new bookings will be placed on lanes above the new count.

### Notifications (Tab 3)

A read-only reference showing which notifications the system sends automatically and through which channels. No configuration is available here — notifications fire on every qualifying event and cannot be toggled off.

**Customer notifications:**

| Event | Channels |
|---|---|
| Booking confirmed | Email + SMS + Calendar invite |
| Booking waitlisted | Email + SMS |
| Booking cancelled | Email + SMS |
| Booking rescheduled | Email + SMS |
| 24-hour reminder | Email |
| 2-hour reminder | SMS |

**Admin notifications:**

| Event | Channels |
|---|---|
| Booking confirmed | Email |
| Booking waitlisted | Email |
| Booking cancelled | Email |
| Booking rescheduled | Email |

SMS is skipped for customers who have opted out. Calendar invites go to customers only. Instructors do not receive automated notifications.

### Cancellation Policy (Tab 4)

A read-only reference showing the cancellation rules currently enforced by the system:

| Rule | Current Value |
|---|---|
| Late Cancellation Window | 24 hours before session |
| Customer Self-Cancellation | Allowed via manage link |
| Cancellation Reason Required | No |
| No-Show Policy | Customers who miss a session without cancelling are marked No-Show after 15 minutes |

### Security (Tab 5)

- **Change Password** — form with current password, new password, confirm new password. *(Note: In the current prototype the password change is not persisted — the hardcoded admin password remains active after saving.)*
- Shows the current admin username.
