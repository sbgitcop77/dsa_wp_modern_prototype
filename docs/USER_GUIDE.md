# 1. For Athletes & Families

## 1.1 Booking a Session

Go to **Book a Session** on the website (header or any CTA button). The booking process has 5 steps.

### Step 1 — Choose an Instructor

Browse the available coaches and click **Select** on the one you'd like to train with. Each card shows the coach's name, speciality, and a short bio.

**Tip:** If a coach is not shown, they may not be active. Contact us at **(443) 865-1639** if you have a specific request.

### Step 2 — Select Date & Time

1. **Pick a date** from the calendar. Only dates when your chosen coach is available are shown. Greyed-out dates are either in the past, a facility closure, or a date when the coach is unavailable.
2. **Select duration** — 30 minutes or 60 minutes. Choose this first; the available time slots update based on your choice.
3. **Pick a time slot** from the list. Only slots that fit within the coach's session window for that day are shown.

### Step 3 — Session Frequency

Choose how often you'd like to train:

| Option | Description |
|---|---|
| **Single Session** | One session on the date and time you selected. |
| **Recurring Series** | The same day and time every week for 2, 4, 6, or 8 weeks. |

For recurring sessions, we check each future date automatically. See §1.2 for details.

Click **Next** to continue. If there is a problem with any date in your series, you'll see a message here before proceeding.

### Step 4 — Participant Details

Tell us who the session is for:

- **Myself** — you are the athlete. Fill in your first name, last name, email, and phone number.
- **A Child** — you're booking on behalf of a child. Fill in the child's name, age, your relationship to them, and your own contact details.

Your email is used to look up your account. If you've booked before, your details are already on file.

### Step 5 — Review & Confirm

Review all the details. If everything looks right, click **Confirm Booking**.

You'll see a confirmation screen with your **booking reference** (format: `DSA-YYYY-NNNNN`). Save this — you'll need it to manage your booking later. A confirmation email will be sent to the address you provided.

## 1.2 Recurring (Weekly) Sessions

When you choose **Recurring Series**, we automatically check every upcoming date in your series before you confirm. Here's what can happen:

### All weeks clear
Every date in your series is available → all sessions are booked as **Confirmed**. Your confirmation screen shows each session (Session 1 of N, Session 2 of N, etc.) with its date and a link to manage it.

### Facility closed on a future date (hard block)
If one of your weekly dates falls on a facility closure (holiday, maintenance day, etc.), you'll see a **red alert** on Step 3 listing the affected date(s). You cannot proceed with that number of weeks. Options:
- Reduce the number of weeks so the series ends before the closure.
- Start your series on a different base date.
- Choose a single session instead.

### Coach conflict on a future date (waitlisted)
If your coach is already booked by another athlete on one of your weekly dates (but the facility itself is open), that session is placed on the **waitlist** rather than blocking your entire series. You'll see an **amber notice** on Step 3 showing which date is affected. You can still proceed — the other sessions are confirmed, and the waitlisted session is created in your account.

See §1.8 for details on what waitlisted means and how to confirm it.

## 1.3 Booking for a Child

On Step 4 (Participant Details), select **A Child — I am booking on their behalf**.

You'll need to provide:
- Child's **first and last name**
- Child's **age**
- Your **relationship** to the child (e.g. Parent, Guardian, Coach)
- Your own **first name, last name, email, and phone** as the booking contact

All communications go to the contact email provided.

## 1.4 Managing Your Booking

Every booking has a dedicated management page. Navigate to:

```
https://thediamondsportsacademy.com/manage/[your-booking-reference]
```

Replace `[your-booking-reference]` with the reference in your confirmation (e.g. `DSA-2026-00001`). No login is required.

On this page you can:
- View full booking details (coach, date, time, duration, status)
- Cancel the session
- Reschedule to a different date or time
- For recurring series: see all sessions in your series and their current status
- For waitlisted sessions: confirm the session when the slot opens

---

## 1.5 Cancelling a Session

From your booking management page:

1. Click **Cancel — Remove this session**
2. For recurring series, choose:
   - **This session only** — only this date is cancelled; all other sessions remain as scheduled.
   - **Entire series (all upcoming sessions)** — this and all future sessions in the series are cancelled.
3. Add an optional reason.
4. Click **Confirm Cancellation**.

You'll see a confirmation message. A cancellation email is sent to your contact address.

### 24-Hour Change Window

Cancellations and reschedules **must be made more than 24 hours before your session**. If your session is within 24 hours, the change options are locked and you'll see a "Change Window Closed" notice with instructions to contact us directly:

> **Phone:** (443) 865-1639
> **Hours:** Mon–Fri 8 AM–8 PM, Sat 9 AM–5 PM

**Exception:** If your session is **waitlisted** (not yet confirmed), you can still confirm it within 24 hours — the time lock only applies to confirmed sessions.

---

## 1.6 Rescheduling a Session

From your booking management page:

1. Click **Reschedule — Pick a new date & time**
2. Select a new date from the calendar. The calendar shows only dates when your coach is available (blackout dates and dates with no coach availability are disabled).
3. Select a new time slot. Slots already booked by your coach, slots that don't fit within the session window, and slots where you'd extend past the coach's available hours are automatically excluded.
4. Click **Confirm Reschedule**.

**Note:** The session duration (30 or 60 minutes) cannot be changed during a reschedule. Contact us if you need to switch durations.

**Note for recurring series:** The manage page reschedules a single occurrence only. Rescheduling an entire series is not available online — contact us to arrange that.

## 1.7 Understanding Your Booking Status

| Status | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Confirmed** | Your session is booked and confirmed. Show up at the scheduled time. |
| **Waitlisted** | Your session exists in our system but the exact time slot has a temporary conflict (coach is booked by another athlete that week). You need to confirm it once the slot opens. See §1.8. |
| **Cancelled** | The session was cancelled by you or by our staff. |
| **Completed** | The session took place. |
| **No Show** | The session date passed without attendance. |

---

## 1.8 Waitlisted Sessions

A session becomes **Waitlisted** when it is part of a recurring series and the coach happens to already have a booking on one of your weekly dates. Rather than blocking your entire series, we create the session in a waitlisted state so you have a placeholder.

**What you can do:**

1. **Wait** — if the conflicting booking gets cancelled, our staff may reach out to let you know the slot is available.
2. **Confirm it yourself** — go to your booking management page for the waitlisted session. If the slot is now free, you'll see a green **"Slot Available — Confirm Now"** banner and a **"Confirm This Session"** button. Click it to lock in the session.
3. **Reschedule it** — use the Reschedule option to move the session to a different available date/time.
4. **Cancel it** — if you no longer need it, cancel the single session without affecting your other series sessions.

**Important:** A waitlisted session is NOT guaranteed until you or our staff confirms it. The 24-hour change window does not apply — you can confirm a waitlisted session at any time.

## 1.9 Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Can I book if I don't have an account?**
A: Yes. The first time you book, your email creates a customer record automatically. Future bookings with the same email are linked to your account.

**Q: What if the coach I want isn't available on the date I need?**
A: Each coach sets their own availability schedule. Try a different date, or choose another coach. You can also contact us directly and we'll do our best to accommodate.

**Q: Can I change the duration during a reschedule?**
A: Not online. Contact us at (443) 865-1639 to change the duration on an existing booking.

**Q: What happens if the facility is closed on one of my recurring session dates?**
A: You'll be shown the blocked date during booking (Step 3) and prevented from confirming. Adjust your number of weeks or start date to avoid the closure.

**Q: Can I book a recurring series that includes holidays?**
A: Only if the holiday is not a designated facility closure. If a session date lands on a blackout date (facility closure), you must adjust the series to avoid it.

**Q: What if I have a waitlisted session but the slot never opens?**
A: You can reschedule the waitlisted session to any other available date, or cancel it without affecting your other series sessions.

**Q: How do I know my cancellation went through?**
A: You'll see a confirmation message on screen and receive a cancellation email. You can also revisit your booking management page — the status will show as "Cancelled".

**Q: What is the cancellation policy?**
A: Cancellations and reschedules must be made more than 24 hours in advance. Within 24 hours, please contact us directly.

# 2. For Admin Staff

Access the admin panel at `/admin`. You must be logged in.

## 2.1 Logging In

Go to `/admin/login`. Enter your admin username and password.

After logging in you are taken to the admin dashboard. Your session persists until you click **Sign out** (top right of every admin page).

## 2.2 Dashboard — Today's Schedule

The dashboard shows a live grid of all sessions scheduled for **today**. Columns represent the facility's lanes; each booking appears as a block at its scheduled time.

**What you can do here:**
- Get a quick overview of who's training today and when.
- Spot open lanes at a glance.
- Click a booking block to view its details.

The grid runs from **6 AM to 9:30 PM**. Sessions for the current day that are confirmed or waitlisted are shown.

## 2.3 Bookings

Go to **Bookings** in the sidebar.

### Tabs

| Tab | Shows |
|---|---|
| Upcoming | All bookings with a date on or after today |
| Past | All bookings with a date before today |

### Filters

Narrow the list by:
- **Instructor** — filter by a specific coach
- **Status** — Confirmed, Waitlisted, Cancelled, No Show, Completed
- **Date range** — from date / to date

### Booking Detail Modal

Click any row to open the detail modal. It shows:
- Reference number, customer name, instructor, date, time, duration, lane (if lane instructor)
- Status badge — amber for Waitlisted with the conflict reason shown
- Session type (single / recurring series)

**Actions in the modal:**

| Button | When it appears | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Reschedule | status = confirmed | Opens the reschedule form |
| Cancel Booking | status = confirmed | Cancels the booking (records cancelledBy: admin) |
| Confirm Session | status = waitlisted | Promotes the booking to confirmed, clears conflict reason |

### Creating a Walk-In Booking

Walk-ins are created from the admin panel only. Look for the **+ New Booking** or **Walk-In** button (if available in your version). Walk-in bookings follow the same lane conflict and availability rules as online bookings.

### Exporting

Use the **Export CSV** button to download a CSV of the currently-filtered bookings.

## 2.4 Customers

Go to **Customers** in the sidebar.

The list shows all customer records with their contact info, booking history counts, and status indicators.

### Customer Flags

| Indicator | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Flagged** | Customer has been flagged by admin. They cannot book online. |
| **Inactive** | Customer account is deactivated. They cannot book online. |

### Actions

Click a customer to open their detail panel. From there you can:
- **Flag / Unflag** — toggle the flag status. A flagged customer is blocked from making new online bookings.
- **Deactivate / Reactivate** — deactivating records the deactivation timestamp. Existing bookings are NOT automatically cancelled — review them separately.
- **Edit details** — update name, phone, email.
- **Increment no-show count** — record a no-show manually. If `noShowCount` reaches the threshold (default: 3), auto-flag may be applied.
- **Increment late cancellation count** — record a late cancellation.

**Note:** A customer's email is their unique identifier. Changing their email should be done with care — it will affect future booking lookups.

## 2.5 Instructors

Go to **Instructors** in the sidebar.

### Instructor List

Each card shows:
- Photo (placeholder if none), name, speciality
- Stats: total sessions delivered, upcoming sessions (these are computed from actual bookings)
- Type: Lane or Non-Lane
- Status: Active / Inactive

### Creating an Instructor

Click **+ Add Instructor**. All fields are required:
- First Name, Last Name
- Email (must be unique)
- Phone
- Speciality (e.g. "Pitching & Conditioning")
- **Instructor Type** — either **Lane** or **Non-Lane**. This cannot be changed after creation without admin intervention.

**Lane Instructor:** Sessions assigned to a physical batting lane. Lane capacity checks apply. The lane number appears in booking details.

**Non-Lane Instructor:** Sessions do not consume a batting lane (e.g. speed/agility coaching). Lane capacity checks do not apply to these sessions.

### Editing an Instructor

Click the edit icon on the instructor card. You can update all fields except Instructor Type (contact dev team to change this as it has booking implications).

### Deactivating an Instructor

Deactivated instructors no longer appear in the public booking wizard. Their historical bookings remain intact. **Existing confirmed bookings are not cancelled automatically** — review the instructor's upcoming bookings in the Bookings tab and handle them manually.

## 2.6 Instructor Availability

Go to **Instructors** → click the **Availability** button on an instructor card.

### Recurring Weekly Template

Set the instructor's default weekly schedule. For each day of the week, toggle it on/off and set the start and end time. This is the fallback schedule used on dates that have no specific override.

### Date-Specific Overrides

The calendar view shows the current month. Each date has a card showing the instructor's availability for that day (whether it's from the weekly template or a custom override).

**To override a date:**
1. Click the date card.
2. Toggle the instructor as available or unavailable for that date.
3. If available: set the start and end time.
4. Save.

**Rules:**
- You cannot edit past dates.
- You cannot set a start time in the past for today.
- End time must be after start time.
- Blackout dates and facility-closed days are shown in red and cannot be edited.
- Saturday hours must fall within 9 AM–5 PM (Saturday facility hours).

**Apply to all active days:** Copies the current date's start/end time to all other active, editable dates in the same calendar month.

**Frozen dates:** If a date shows a lock icon, it is frozen and cannot be edited through the normal flow. Contact your system admin to unfreeze.

## 2.7 Waitlist

Go to **Waitlist** in the sidebar.

The waitlist here is the **slot-notification waitlist** — customers who asked to be notified when a specific slot opens up. This is separate from `waitlisted` bookings (which already have a booking in the system).

### Waitlist Entries

Each entry shows the customer name (click to open a detail card with email, phone, instructor, date, time, duration, and waitlist reason), the requested date/time/duration, their status, why they are waiting (Lane Unavailable or Instructor Unavailable), and when they were added.

Use the **Upcoming / Past** tabs to switch timeframes and the search box to filter by customer name.

### Unified Waitlist

The waitlist page shows two types of entries:

- **Lane Unavailable** — customer used the public booking wizard but all lanes were full; they requested a notification. No booking record exists yet.
- **Instructor Unavailable** — customer booked a recurring series where a future session conflicts with the instructor's schedule; the booking record exists with status `waitlisted`.

### Promoting a Waitlist Entry (Lane Unavailable)

When a slot opens, find the entry and click **Promote**. A confirmation modal summarises the action; click **Promote to Booking** to proceed. The system creates a confirmed booking, assigns an available lane, and records a confirmation notification for the customer. The waitlist entry is then removed.

### Confirming a Waitlisted Booking (Instructor Unavailable)

When the instructor becomes available, click **Confirm**. A confirmation modal appears; click **Confirm Booking** to proceed. The booking status changes to `confirmed` and a confirmation notification is recorded. The entry is removed from the waitlist view.

### Removing an Entry

If a waitlist entry is no longer needed, click **Remove**. For Lane Unavailable entries the waitlist record is deleted; for Instructor Unavailable entries the booking is cancelled.

## 2.8 Settings

Go to **Settings** in the sidebar. Settings are divided into sections:

### Facility Information
Update the facility's name, address, phone, email, and website. These appear on public-facing pages and notification emails.

### Operating Hours
Set the facility's open and close times for each day of the week. Toggle any day as closed. Rules:
- Close time must be after open time.
- Closed days block all bookings and instructor availability for that weekday.
- Saturday is a working day by default (9 AM–5 PM). Sunday is closed by default.

### Blackout Dates
Manage facility-wide closure dates.

**To add a blackout:**
1. Click **+ Add Blackout Date**.
2. Choose the date.
3. Enter a reason (e.g. "Annual Deep Clean").
4. Toggle **Recurring annually** if this closure repeats every year on the same calendar date (e.g. a holiday).
5. Save.

**To remove a blackout:** Click the delete icon. This immediately makes the date available for bookings again.

**Important:** Waitlisted bookings cannot exist on a blackout date — when a blackout is added, any waitlisted or confirmed bookings on that date are cancelled before the blackout is saved. Removing a blackout simply makes the date available for new bookings again.

**Note:** You cannot add blackout dates in the past.

**Recurring blackouts** match by month and day only — the year is ignored. For example, a recurring blackout on "2026-07-04" will also block July 4 in 2027, 2028, and every future year.

### Lane Count
The **Active Lanes** setting controls how many simultaneous lane sessions can be booked at the same time slot. Reducing this number will not cancel existing bookings, but will prevent new bookings at already-full time slots.

**When reducing the lane count**, if there are confirmed upcoming bookings assigned to lanes above the new count, a conflict warning appears before saving. The warning lists every affected booking (reference, customer, date, and lane number). You have two options:

- **Cancel (keep current count)** — dismisses the warning, no changes are saved. Use this to go back and review the bookings first.
- **Save Anyway** — saves the new lane count immediately. The listed bookings remain confirmed but will now be on lanes that exceed the configured capacity. You must manually review and handle them in the Bookings tab.

New bookings are always capped at the active lane count, so no further over-capacity bookings can be created after saving.

## 2.9 Notifications

Go to **Notifications** in the sidebar.

This is a log of all notifications the system has sent (or attempted to send). Each entry shows:
- Type (confirmation, reminder, cancellation, etc.)
- Channel (email, SMS, calendar)
- Recipient (customer, instructor, admin)
- Sent time
- Delivery status: **Sent**, **Pending**, or **Failed**

**Note:** In the current prototype, notifications are recorded but not actually sent. In production, this log reflects real email and SMS delivery.

**Types:**

| Type | When it fires |
|---|---|
| Confirmation | New booking created and confirmed; waitlisted booking confirmed by admin |
| Waitlist | Booking placed on waitlist at creation (instructor conflict) |
| Change | Booking rescheduled (by customer or admin) |
| Cancellation | Booking cancelled (by customer or admin, single or entire series) |
| Reminder (24hr) | 24 hours before session — **not yet automated; requires scheduled job** |
| Reminder (2hr SMS) | 2 hours before session — **not yet automated; requires scheduled job** |

**Channel rules per recipient:**

| Event | Customer channels | Admin channels |
|---|---|---|
| Confirmation | Email + SMS + Calendar invite | Email only |
| Waitlist | Email + SMS | Email only |
| Cancellation | Email + SMS | Email only |
| Change / Reschedule | Email + SMS | Email only |

> **Why no calendar invite for admin?** The admin already has a full real-time view of all bookings through Today's Schedule and Manage Bookings. A calendar invite per booking would flood the admin's personal calendar. The application itself serves as the admin's scheduling tool. Calendar invites are sent to customers only — they have no app access and need the invite as a personal reminder on their own device.

> **SMS opt-out:** If a customer has opted out of SMS, the SMS channel is skipped for all notification types. The remaining channels still fire as normal.

**Actions that create notifications:**
- Customer books a confirmed session (public wizard) → confirmation per booking
- Customer books a waitlisted session (instructor conflict) → waitlist per booking
- Customer cancels on the manage page → cancellation
- Customer reschedules on the manage page → change
- Admin cancels a booking (Bookings page or Today's Schedule) → cancellation
- Admin reschedules a booking → change
- Admin confirms a waitlisted booking → confirmation
- Admin deactivates a customer → cancellation per affected future booking
